JV Lead Submission Criteria

Find your MAO

  1. Take the ARV.
  2. Subtract the repair cost.
  3. Multiply by 0.75.
  4. Subtract $7,500.

Example

ARV: $300,000
Repairs: $30,000
$300,000 − $30,000 = $270,000
$270,000 × 0.75 = $202,500
$202,500 − $7,500 = MAO $195,000
Highest asking price that passes: about $216,600

Deal types we accept: Assignment, Flip
